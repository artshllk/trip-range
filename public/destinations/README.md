These responsive WebP derivatives retain the licences of their Commons originals.
They are resized and may be cropped for display. No endorsement is implied.

Author, source, licence and modification credit accompany the displayed images.
Reuse of these adapted photographs must retain attribution and the applicable
share-alike licence. Approval applies to the pinned hero/card roles and crops;
other placements or changed bytes require the existing review process.

`content/media/approved.json` records original Commons identities/checksums,
local derivative dimensions, byte sizes and SHA-256 values, and the separately
reviewed delivery pins. The offline media check verifies public files during
prebuild; runtime does not discover or approve replacements.
