# Trip Range data

Our studies report what travellers can learn from recorded fares and official statistics. Each edition includes the source, scope, calculations and files needed to check its claims.

## Available studies

- [Can a national price index predict a city’s hotel rate?](/data/municipal-hotel-scaling/2025-v1) We compared Madrid and Helsinki with 125 other municipalities using monthly hotel rates for 2025. Download the source data and run both holdouts.
- [Two-night trips show 31% to 76% fewer destinations in cached flight data](/data/fare-coverage/2026-11-05-v1) We compared two-night stays with two-to-four-night stays from Tirana, Prishtina and Skopje. Inspect the public aggregates, queries and checks.
