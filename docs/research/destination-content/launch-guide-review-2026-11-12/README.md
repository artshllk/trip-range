### Vienna · [candidate](vienna.json)

**Café Prückel** (26 Nov). Café Prückel example: €51.00. Menu checked 12 November 2026. One venue’s example, not a daily Vienna food budget. Items: Viennese breakfast €9.80, cappuccino €4.90, cheese toast €8.90, beef goulash with dumplings €19.80, and two 0.5-litre sodas at €3.80 each. [source](https://www.prueckel.at/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/Prueckel_Karte_105x260_2026_online_DE.pdf)

**VIE airport works** (12 Dec). During the works, the S7 from Vienna Airport ends at St. Marx. For Hauptbahnhof, take the REX7; for Wien Mitte, CAT runs a replacement bus through 31 October 2027. Check your travel-day timetable. [source 1](https://www.wien.info/en/travel-info/arrival-departure/airport-to-center/important-information-for-airport-travellers-2026-2027-1118558) · [source 2](https://www.wien.info/en/travel-info/arrival-departure/airport-to-center/express-train-s7-345644)

**Core-zone fares** (12 Dec). Choose tickets around the journeys you expect to make. Adult options include a €3.20 single, a €10.20 24-hour ticket and a €25.20 digital seven-day ticket for the Vienna core zone. [source](https://www.wien.info/en/travel-info/transport/tickets-361360)

**Short stay and airport supplement** (12 Dec). For a short stay, compare the single and 24-hour tickets before buying seven days. On the S7 or REX7, an adult who already holds a valid Vienna core-zone ticket needs a €2.30 supplement to reach the airport. [source 1](https://www.wien.info/en/travel-info/transport/tickets-361360) · [source 2](https://www.wien.info/en/travel-info/arrival-departure/airport-to-center/express-train-s7-345644)

**Dishoom Covent Garden** (26 Nov). Dishoom Covent Garden example: £55.00 before optional 12.5% service (about £62 with it), removable on request. Menus checked 12 November 2026. One venue’s example, not a daily London food budget. Items: date and banana porridge £9.50, house chai £4.90, paneer roomali roll £12.90, chole puri halwa £15.90, and two fresh nimbu sodas at £5.90 each. [source 1](https://www.dishoom.com/menu/breakfast-covent-garden/) · [source 2](https://www.dishoom.com/menu/covent-garden-all-day/) · [source 3](https://cdn.sanity.io/files/daku84np/production/ddd9a3df5c185e38227ec34cfd3b0e606ece0ef5.pdf) · [source 4](https://cdn.sanity.io/files/daku84np/production/828436b12fba73190f349fb540d46f4c0a6c21bc.pdf)

**Westminster Abbey** (26 Nov). Westminster Abbey recommends booking sightseeing tickets ahead. Sundays are for services, so choose another day for sightseeing and check the Abbey’s calendar before planning your visit. [source](https://www.westminster-abbey.org/visit-us)

**Zones 1–2 daily cap** (12 Dec). Pay as you go charges for the journeys you take. Qualifying adult travel within Zones 1–2 is capped at £8.90 per fare day when you use the same card or device correctly; fewer journeys can cost less. [source 1](https://content.tfl.gov.uk/adult-fares.pdf) · [source 2](https://tfl.gov.uk/fares/find-fares/caps-and-travelcard-prices)

**Airport and outer-zone exceptions** (12 Dec). Do not budget for an airport trip using the Zones 1–2 cap. Travelling into other zones can mean a higher cap, and Heathrow Express is outside the cap altogether. [source 1](https://content.tfl.gov.uk/adult-fares.pdf) · [source 2](https://tfl.gov.uk/fares/find-fares/caps-and-travelcard-prices)

### Paris · [candidate](paris.json)

**Bouillon Chartier** (26 Nov). Bouillon Chartier lists main courses at €7–€13.80 on its indicative menu, checked 12 November 2026. Dishes change daily with deliveries. This is one restaurant’s published range, not a daily Paris food budget. [source 1](https://www.bouillon-chartier.com/chartier_medias/2025/10/Francais.pdf) · [source 2](https://www.bouillon-chartier.com/la-carte/)

At the tower, choose stairs or lift to the second floor; the final leg to the summit is by lift. [source 1](https://www.louvre.fr/en/visit/hours-admission/tickets-and-prices) · [source 2](https://www.toureiffel.paris/en/rates-opening-times)

**Navigo Jour** (12 Dec). Compare single tickets with a day pass for the journeys you plan. Navigo Jour costs €12.30 for a calendar day across Zones 1–5, but excludes airport access. A compatible phone avoids needing to buy a physical pass. [source](https://www.ratp.fr/en/titres-et-tarifs/forfait-navigo-jour)

## Page order for review only

After the title and a short introduction, put cost and logistics first: a priced transit choice, the airport route and any active disruption, then a dated venue example where one exists. Put neighbourhoods next, sights and their access notes after that, then the day outline as the last editorial section; keep sources as a footer. Barcelona and Istanbul should lead with the airport route rather than an empty price block. Paris has no day template.

No change to the content schema is needed for this release. The destination guide composes independent transport, practical, area, attraction and day sections. Moving those sections and their jump links is a guide-template change. To show only costs/logistics at the top, select existing reviewed notes by editorial item ID; simply moving all practical notes also promotes history and general booking advice. Select only from the freshness-filtered result, keep checked dates and official source links with the facts, do not duplicate notes below, and omit empty sections. Nothing about trip-result templates, budgets or price calculations needs to change.
