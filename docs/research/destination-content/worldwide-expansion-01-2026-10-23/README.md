## Evidence and limits that affect publication

Research used official tourism bodies, public authorities, airport/transport operators and attraction custodians. New copy is an editorial paraphrase, not copied promotional text, a personal visit or a hotel review. “Retrieved” means checked during this research session, not the source's publication date. Some evidence was available as indexed excerpts of the primary page rather than a complete rendered page; that is not a live service-status test. Existing identities and approved photos retain their earlier evidence and review history.

- **Istanbul:** recheck Hagia Sophia tourist entrance/ticket scope. The draft quotes no admission price, opening schedule or guaranteed access. T1, IST and SAW retain their separate meanings.
- **Bangkok:** a September DMK advisory concerns disruption, not a permanent service rule. Recheck the operator before approval; the draft assumes no taxi availability and quotes no hours.
- **Singapore:** the National Gallery's own site lists the UOB Southeast Asia Gallery as closed. The draft does not promise that collection. Garden maintenance is date-specific, not a general closure of all gardens.
- **Cape Town:** MyCiTi's explicit airport-service suspension outranks old timetable text on the same page and older tourism summaries. Use the current airport operator's authorised ground-transport guidance. Recheck the suspension before publication.
- **Rio:** Parque Lage's operator marks the mansion closed for restoration. No interior/courtyard visit is promised. RRJ ground transport remains unverified; GIG/SDU directions must not be applied to RRJ.
- **Sydney:** WSI passenger opening is scheduled for 25 October 2026, after this research date. Bus/metro arrangements are plans, not observed operations. Recheck after opening; SYD's T8 and station fee are separate.
- **Dubai:** the DWC operator page contains inconsistent sample interchange names. The draft accurately requires a bus/taxi-to-metro connection without prescribing one disputed station.
- **Mexico City:** official Templo Mayor price figures conflict. No ticket amount is proposed. Inaccessible original museum pages were replaced with relevant federal/city sources, not assumed current from a failed fetch.

These are editorial evidence gaps/caveats, not newly demonstrated product failures. Sources can establish the visitor decision without establishing a current fare, hotel inventory or every airport's full route. No flight, OpenAI or Redis request was made.

All numerical accommodation, meal, local-travel, attraction and airport-transfer profiles remain unchanged. The packet activates **zero** prices. Generic existing planning allowances must not be relabelled as researched city evidence. Unknown required costs remain unknown, reserves remain separate and native-currency evidence cannot silently become EUR. Short-stay templates are optional editorial sequences, not guaranteed opening-day schedules or sold packages.

General guidance is proposed for recheck by **21 January 2027**. Particularly operational items carry the shorter **6 November 2026** review deadline. Every operational statement must be rechecked before the owner approves publication, even if its stored deadline has not arrived. This is a proposed maintenance policy, not a promise of automated monitoring.
