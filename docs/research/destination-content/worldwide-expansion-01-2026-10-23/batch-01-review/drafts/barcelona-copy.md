# Barcelona — English v3, revised batch 1 candidate

DRAFT — not approved or published. Packet: worldwide-batch1-review-v1.

Barcelona works well when you plan by neighbourhood: Gaudí buildings in the Eixample, the lanes of the old city and art museums on separate outings. Choose the interiors that matter to you before adding up tickets.

## Worth seeing

### Sagrada Família
See Gaudí’s basilica as a planned interior visit rather than assuming you can walk straight in. Check the official visitor options for your date.

### Park Güell
Combine Gaudí’s designed spaces with a park outing. Check current works and ticket arrangements before deciding which parts to visit.

### Casa Batlló
Look for modernista detail on Passeig de Gràcia. An exterior walk and an interior visit are different choices for your budget.

### Museu Picasso
Use the collection to explore Picasso’s formative years; it offers a different focus from a Gaudí architecture day.

### Museu Nacional d’Art de Catalunya
See Romanesque mural paintings and Catalan modern art in the Palau Nacional on Montjuïc. Treat it as a separate outing from the old-city Picasso collection.

## Choosing a base

### Eixample
Consider a base near a useful metro station for architecture outings and cross-city travel.
Good for: Gaudí buildings and a street-grid base.
Consider: Check the exact block and station; Eixample is a large district.

### Gothic Quarter
Consider the old city if historic lanes are the main reason for your visit.
Good for: Exploring the Roman and medieval centre on foot.
Consider: Check the property entrance, luggage access and noise conditions directly.

## 2 nights in Barcelona

- Day 1: **Casa Batlló** — Start with the exterior and the surrounding Eixample streets if arrival leaves time.
- Day 2: **Sagrada Família** — Use your confirmed entry arrangement; leave time to enjoy the interior.
- Day 2: **Park Güell** — Make a separate connection to the park; do not assume the two Gaudí sites are adjacent.
- Day 3: **Museu Picasso** — An old-city museum visit when open; move it off Monday and leave enough time before departure.

Suggested order, not a booked package. Arrival and departure days may be short: move or omit stops to fit your actual times. Check current access and tickets; do not rush to complete every option.

## Getting around

### Arriving at Barcelona–El Prat (BCN)
L9 Sud serves both airport terminals and connects with other metro lines. Compare the connection to your accommodation, not just the airport station. Some ordinary tickets are not valid at the airport metro gates.

### Getting between neighbourhoods
Group the Eixample buildings together and plan a separate old-city outing. Park Güell needs its own approach; check TMB’s route information rather than assuming every sight is an easy walk.

## Before you go

The Gothic Quarter follows the Roman and medieval core. The later Eixample grid gave the expanding city room for the modernista buildings that still shape its streets.

Interior tickets, local journeys and airport travel are separate choices. This guide supplies no reviewed accommodation, meal or complete attraction budget. Check the actual property total and chosen tickets.

Museu Picasso closes on Mondays. MNAC normally does too, with public-holiday exceptions. Move the museum day if needed and check your chosen date.
