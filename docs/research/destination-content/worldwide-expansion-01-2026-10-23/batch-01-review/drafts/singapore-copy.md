Singapore offers gardens, art and heritage districts without needing a new base every day. Choose which garden interiors and museum collections matter to you, then connect the outings by public transport rather than treating every attraction as one walk.

## Worth seeing

### Gardens by the Bay
Choose the outdoor spaces and any ticketed attractions separately. Check the official closure notices before selecting a conservatory visit.

### National Gallery Singapore
Explore art in the former City Hall and Supreme Court. The UOB Southeast Asia Gallery is temporarily closed; other collections remain open.

### Singapore Botanic Gardens
Explore the gardens’ paths and lakes. The National Orchid Garden has separate admission; choose your entrance from the official map.

### Buddha Tooth Relic Temple and Museum
Visit this Tang-inspired Buddhist temple in Chinatown respectfully. Check which visitor spaces are accessible on your date.

## Choosing a base

### Chinatown
Consider Chinatown for heritage streets and the temple, checking the station and walking route for your accommodation.
Good for: Exploring the surrounding Chinatown area between planned visits.
Consider: The Botanic Gardens and Marina Bay are separate outings, not one neighbourhood walk.

### Civic District and Marina Bay
Compare the exact location if art museums or the bay are your main priorities.
Good for: Staying close to your chosen central-city outing.
Consider: The district name does not establish walking distance to every garden entrance.

## Getting around

### Arriving at Changi (SIN)
Changi’s rail connection uses changes at Tanah Merah or Expo for onward city travel. Follow the airport’s terminal-specific instructions and check current service notices, especially for a late arrival.

### Arriving at Seletar (XSP)
Seletar is not Changi. Its operator lists bus 102 with North East Line connections, as well as taxi and private-hire options. Plan this airport leg separately from any SIN guidance.

### Between the outings
Use LTA’s journey planner for the exact station and garden entrance. Group the gallery with the Civic District, and keep the Botanic Gardens separate from the Marina Bay outing.

## Before you go

The National Gallery occupies the former City Hall and Supreme Court. Chinatown’s Buddha Tooth Relic Temple is a modern Tang-inspired building, not an ancient monument.

Choose garden attractions and museum tickets separately. Hotel costs, meals and airport journeys still need checking; this guide does not supply a complete trip price.
