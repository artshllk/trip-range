## Overview

Sydney’s harbour gives a short stay a clear focus: the Opera House, garden, bridge and The Rocks can anchor your plan. Choose a base and airport connection before adding more distant outings or paid experiences.

Sources: [Our story](https://www.therocks.com/our-story); [Tubowgule](https://www.sydneyoperahouse.com/our-story/tubowgule); [Sydney attractions](https://www.sydney.com/articles/top-attractions-in-sydney); [Royal Botanic Garden visitor guide](https://www.botanicgardens.org.au/royal-botanic-garden-sydney/plan-your-visit)

## History and identity

The harbour is Gadigal Country, with a history far older than the colonial buildings of The Rocks. Tubowgule, now Bennelong Point, was a gathering place before the Opera House; read that history alongside the familiar skyline.

Sources: [Our story](https://www.therocks.com/our-story); [Tubowgule](https://www.sydneyoperahouse.com/our-story/tubowgule)

## Arrival and local transport

### Sydney Airport (SYD)

T8 serves the domestic and international airport stations. The station access fee is additional to the train fare and is outside ordinary Opal daily/weekly travel caps. Use the official planner for your exact terminal and destination.

Sources: [Getting to Sydney Airport](https://transportnsw.info/travel-info/using-public-transport/getting-to-airport)

### Western Sydney International (WSI)

WSI is separate from SYD. Passenger opening is scheduled for 25 October 2026, with buses including a St Marys connection while the metro is under construction. Verify actual operations before travel; do not use SYD directions.

Sources: [Getting to Western Sydney International](https://transportnsw.info/travel-info/airport-travel/getting-to-from-western-sydney-international-airport-wsi); [WSI opening and transport FAQs](https://www.wsiairport.com.au/faqs?page=1)

### Explore from the harbour hub
