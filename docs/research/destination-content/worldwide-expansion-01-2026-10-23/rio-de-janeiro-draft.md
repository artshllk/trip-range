## Overview

Rio combines beach neighbourhoods with hilltop viewpoints and a city centre that needs its own outing. Build your stay around a few journeys, keeping weather-sensitive views flexible and checking the return from each attraction.

Sources: [History of Rio](https://www.rio.rj.gov.br/web/guest/exibeconteudo?article-id=87129); [Parque Lage](https://eavparquelage.rj.gov.br/o-parque)

## History and identity

Rio grew as a port before expanding towards the beach districts. Parque Lage preserves an estate landscape and later cultural history, but its mansion is currently listed as closed for restoration.

Sources: [History of Rio](https://www.rio.rj.gov.br/web/guest/exibeconteudo?article-id=87129); [Parque Lage](https://eavparquelage.rj.gov.br/o-parque)
