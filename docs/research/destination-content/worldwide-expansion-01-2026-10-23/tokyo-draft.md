## Overview

Tokyo is easier to plan as a few neighbourhood outings than a citywide checklist. Pair Asakusa with the Skytree area, and keep Meiji Jingu and Yoyogi Park together on a different day. Your airport and nearest station matter as much as the district name.

Sources: [A guide to Asakusa](https://www.gotokyo.org/en/destinations/eastern-tokyo/asakusa/index.html); [Meiji Shrine](https://www.gotokyo.org/en/spot/76/index.html); [Sumida River Walk area map](https://www.gotokyo.org/book/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/AO_2604_sumidariverwalk_low_EN.pdf)

## History and identity

Sensoji anchors Asakusa’s temple streets. Across the city, Meiji Jingu honours Emperor Meiji and Empress Shōken in a planted forest, giving a short stay two very different views of Tokyo’s history.

Sources: [A guide to Asakusa](https://www.gotokyo.org/en/destinations/eastern-tokyo/asakusa/index.html); [Meiji Shrine](https://www.gotokyo.org/en/spot/76/index.html)

## Worth seeing

### Sensoji and Nakamise

Approach Sensoji through Kaminarimon and the Nakamise shopping street. Leave room to explore the surrounding Asakusa streets rather than treating the gate as the whole visit.

Sources: [A guide to Asakusa](https://www.gotokyo.org/en/destinations/eastern-tokyo/asakusa/index.html)

### Meiji Jingu

Walk through the shrine forest near Harajuku. Respect worshippers and check the shrine’s current visitor guidance.

Sources: [Meiji Shrine](https://www.gotokyo.org/en/spot/76/index.html)

### Yoyogi Park

Take an outdoor break beside the Meiji Jingu area. The park and shrine are separate places, even though they pair well in one outing.

Sources: [Harajuku accessibility route](https://www.sangyo-rodo1.metro.tokyo.lg.jp/tourism/accessible/en/course16_08.html); [Meiji Shrine](https://www.gotokyo.org/en/spot/76/index.html)

### Tokyo Skytree

Choose whether you want the paid observation decks or simply the surrounding riverside outing. Check the official ticket options; skyline visibility is not guaranteed.

Sources: [Tokyo Skytree official site](https://www.tokyo-skytree.jp/); [Sumida River Walk area map](https://www.gotokyo.org/book/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/AO_2604_sumidariverwalk_low_EN.pdf)

### Sumida River Walk

Use the named pedestrian connection between Asakusa and the Skytree side for a river outing. Check current access and choose rail if the walk does not suit you.

Sources: [TOKYO mizumachi / Sumida River Walk](https://www.gotokyo.org/en/spot/1795/index.html)

## Choosing a base

### Asakusa

Compare bases near the station if the temple streets and eastern Tokyo are priorities. May suit: Sensoji and the Sumida-side outing. Tradeoff: Western Tokyo still needs a rail journey; check the airport connection for your terminal.

Sources: [A guide to Asakusa](https://www.gotokyo.org/en/destinations/eastern-tokyo/asakusa/index.html); [Getting to Tokyo](https://www.gotokyo.org/en/plan/getting-to-tokyo/)

### Shinjuku

Consider a station-linked base for rail connections and the western side of the city. May suit: A busy district with dining and transport options. Tradeoff: The large station and different exits make the exact address important, especially with luggage.

Sources: [A guide to Shinjuku](https://www.gotokyo.org/en/destinations/western-tokyo/shinjuku/index.html)

## Arrival and local transport

### Check HND versus NRT

Haneda (HND) and Narita (NRT) have different rail and airport-bus connections. Compare the service to your actual station or hotel and check the final service for your arrival time. A Tokyo city code does not identify your terminal.

Sources: [Getting to Tokyo](https://www.gotokyo.org/en/plan/getting-to-tokyo/)

### Plan by neighbourhood and station

Keep Asakusa and the Skytree area in the same outing. Use rail for the separate Meiji Jingu–Yoyogi outing near Harajuku. Check the station exit and any access needs before choosing a walking route.

Sources: [A guide to Asakusa](https://www.gotokyo.org/en/destinations/eastern-tokyo/asakusa/index.html); [Sumida River Walk area map](https://www.gotokyo.org/book/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/AO_2604_sumidariverwalk_low_EN.pdf); [Harajuku accessibility route](https://www.sangyo-rodo1.metro.tokyo.lg.jp/tourism/accessible/en/course16_08.html)

- Day 1: **Sensoji and Nakamise** — Explore the temple approach only if arrival leaves time.
- Day 2: **Meiji Jingu** — Travel to the Harajuku side for a separate outing. **Yoyogi Park** — Add the adjacent park rather than another cross-city transfer.
