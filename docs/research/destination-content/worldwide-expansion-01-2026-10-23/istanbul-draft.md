## Overview

Istanbul’s historic peninsula puts Byzantine and Ottoman landmarks close enough to explore as one area. Keep the major interiors on separate parts of your plan, and choose your base around the airport connection as well as the sights.

Sources: [Hagia Sophia Grand Mosque](https://www.fatih.bel.tr/en/main/pages/the-hagia-sophia-grand-mosque-ayasofya-i-kebi/578); [Ottoman imperial architecture](https://goturkiye.com/architecture/ottoman-imperial-architecture)

## History and identity

Hagia Sophia’s surviving structure dates from the Byzantine period; Topkapı later served as an Ottoman palace and administrative centre. Their different histories are visible within the same compact sightseeing area.

Sources: [Hagia Sophia Grand Mosque](https://www.fatih.bel.tr/en/main/pages/the-hagia-sophia-grand-mosque-ayasofya-i-kebi/578); [Ottoman imperial architecture](https://goturkiye.com/architecture/ottoman-imperial-architecture)

## Worth seeing

### Ayasofya (Hagia Sophia)

Explore the building’s Byzantine and Ottoman history. Sightseeing arrangements and worship access are different; verify the current visitor entrance before going.

Sources: [Hagia Sophia Grand Mosque](https://www.fatih.bel.tr/en/main/pages/the-hagia-sophia-grand-mosque-ayasofya-i-kebi/578)

### Topkapı Palace

Visit the former Ottoman administrative centre and palace courtyards. Check the official ticket scope before adding optional sections to your plan.

Sources: [Ottoman imperial architecture](https://goturkiye.com/architecture/ottoman-imperial-architecture)

### Basilica Cistern

Go underground to see a surviving Byzantine water-storage structure near Hagia Sophia. Treat it as a separate visit, not part of another landmark’s ticket.

Sources: [Architectural wonders: Basilica Cistern](https://goturkiye.com/architecture/architectural-wonders)

### Blue Mosque (Sultanahmet Camii)

Look for the İznik tilework in this active mosque. Plan sightseeing around worship and follow the visitor guidance on arrival.

Sources: [Blue Mosque](https://goturkiye.com/istanbul/blue-mosque); [Blue Mosque visitor guide](https://www.sultanahmetcami.org/sayfa.php?s=ziyaretci-rehberi)

Consider this area for a stay centred on the historic peninsula. May suit: Keeping the four landmark visits in one part of the city. Tradeoff: Airport journeys still need connections; location near a sight does not mean a direct airport ride.

Compare properties near the T1 corridor if onward tram travel matters to your plan. May suit: Moving between the historic sights and the waterfront tram stops. Tradeoff: Check the exact stop, walking route and luggage access rather than relying on an area label.

Sources: [T1 Kabataş–Bağcılar tram](https://www.metro.istanbul/Hatlarimiz/HatDetay?hat=T1)

## Arrival and local transport

### Arriving at IST

Istanbul Airport (IST) has metro, Havaist coach and IETT bus options. Choose an onward connection for your exact base; the airport metro is not a direct tram to Sultanahmet. Check current routes before departure.

Sources: [Istanbul Airport transport](https://dhmi.gov.tr/Sayfalar/Havalimani/Istanbul/Ulasim.aspx); [T1 Kabataş–Bağcılar tram](https://www.metro.istanbul/Hatlarimiz/HatDetay?hat=T1)

### Arriving at SAW

Sabiha Gökçen (SAW) is a different airport. M4 links it with Kadıköy and connects with Marmaray at Ayrılık Çeşmesi. Plan the onward leg to a European-side base separately.

Sources: [Sabiha Gökçen metro](https://www.sabihagokcen.aero/passengers-and-visitors/transport-and-parking/transportation/metro-en)

### Around the historic peninsula

T1 serves Sultanahmet, Gülhane, Sirkeci and Eminönü. Use the station that fits the entrance you need, then walk locally. Keep prayer restrictions separate from transport planning.

Sources: [T1 Kabataş–Bağcılar tram](https://www.metro.istanbul/Hatlarimiz/HatDetay?hat=T1); [Blue Mosque visitor guide](https://www.sultanahmetcami.org/sayfa.php?s=ziyaretci-rehberi)

## Suggested short-stay outlines

These are proposed editorial sequences, not bookable tours or operator-tested schedules. Short arrival/departure days may require moving or omitting stops. No opening time or journey duration is assumed.

### 2 nights in Istanbul

- Day 1: **Blue Mosque (Sultanahmet Camii)** — An arrival-day visit only when sightseeing access is open.
- Day 2: **Topkapı Palace** — Give the palace the main part of your sightseeing day. **Basilica Cistern** — Add the separate cistern visit only if the day remains comfortable.
- Day 3: **Ayasofya (Hagia Sophia)** — Fit the visitor entrance to your departure time, or swap this with day two.

Sources for places and geography: [Blue Mosque](https://goturkiye.com/istanbul/blue-mosque); [Blue Mosque visitor guide](https://www.sultanahmetcami.org/sayfa.php?s=ziyaretci-rehberi); [Ottoman imperial architecture](https://goturkiye.com/architecture/ottoman-imperial-architecture); [Architectural wonders: Basilica Cistern](https://goturkiye.com/architecture/architectural-wonders); [Hagia Sophia Grand Mosque](https://www.fatih.bel.tr/en/main/pages/the-hagia-sophia-grand-mosque-ayasofya-i-kebi/578)

### 3 nights in Istanbul

- Day 1: **Blue Mosque (Sultanahmet Camii)** — Begin near your base when worship and sightseeing arrangements allow.
- Day 2: **Topkapı Palace** — Keep time for the palace rather than rushing through every interior.
- Day 3: **Ayasofya (Hagia Sophia)** — Check current tourist access.
- Day 4: **Basilica Cistern** — A final separate visit if your departure permits; otherwise move it to day three.
