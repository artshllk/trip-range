## Overview

Singapore offers gardens, art and heritage districts without needing a new base every day. Choose which garden interiors and museum collections matter to you, then connect the outings by public transport rather than treating every attraction as one walk.

Sources: [Architecture and history](https://www.nationalgallery.sg/sg/en/architecture-and-history.html); [Buddha Tooth Relic Temple & Museum](https://www.visitsingapore.com/neighbourhood/featured-neighbourhood/chinatown/buddha-tooth-relic-temple/)

## History and identity

The National Gallery occupies the former City Hall and Supreme Court. Chinatown’s Buddha Tooth Relic Temple is a modern Tang-inspired building, not an ancient monument.

Sources: [Architecture and history](https://www.nationalgallery.sg/sg/en/architecture-and-history.html); [Buddha Tooth Relic Temple & Museum](https://www.visitsingapore.com/neighbourhood/featured-neighbourhood/chinatown/buddha-tooth-relic-temple/)

## Worth seeing

### Gardens by the Bay

Choose the outdoor spaces and any ticketed attractions separately. Check the official closure notices before selecting a conservatory visit.

Sources: [Gardens by the Bay](https://www.gardensbythebay.com.sg/)

### National Gallery Singapore

Explore art in the former civic buildings. The UOB Southeast Asia Gallery is listed as closed; check which collections are open before choosing a visit.

Sources: [Architecture and history](https://www.nationalgallery.sg/sg/en/architecture-and-history.html)

### Singapore Botanic Gardens

Make this a separate garden outing using the official map and visitor notices. It is not the same attraction as Gardens by the Bay.

Sources: [Singapore Botanic Gardens](https://sbg.nparks.gov.sg/)

### Buddha Tooth Relic Temple and Museum

Visit this Tang-inspired Buddhist temple in Chinatown respectfully. Check which visitor spaces are accessible on your date.

Sources: [Buddha Tooth Relic Temple & Museum](https://www.visitsingapore.com/neighbourhood/featured-neighbourhood/chinatown/buddha-tooth-relic-temple/)

## Arrival and local transport

### Arriving at Changi (SIN)

Changi’s rail connection uses changes at Tanah Merah or Expo for onward city travel. Follow the airport’s terminal-specific instructions and check current service notices, especially for a late arrival.

Sources: [Leaving Changi Airport](https://www.changiairport.com/en/at-changi/transport-and-directions/leaving-the-airport.html)

### Arriving at Seletar (XSP)

Seletar is not Changi. Its operator lists bus 102 with North East Line connections, as well as taxi and private-hire options. Plan this airport leg separately from any SIN guidance.

Sources: [Seletar transport](https://www.seletarairport.com/transport.html)

### Between the outings

Use LTA’s journey planner for the exact station and garden entrance. Group the gallery with the Civic District, and keep the Botanic Gardens separate from the Marina Bay outing.

Sources: [Plan Your Journey](https://www.lta.gov.sg/content/ltagov/en/getting_around/public_transport/plan_your_journey.html); [Featured neighbourhoods](https://www.visitsingapore.com/neighbourhood/featured-neighbourhood/); [Singapore Botanic Gardens](https://sbg.nparks.gov.sg/); [Gardens by the Bay](https://www.gardensbythebay.com.sg/)
