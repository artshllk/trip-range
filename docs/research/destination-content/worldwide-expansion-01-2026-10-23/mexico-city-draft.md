## Overview

Mexico City is easier to explore by grouping the historic centre separately from Chapultepec. Choose a few museum visits, leave room for the surrounding streets and check the airport before deciding how much arrival time the plan needs.

Sources: [Templo Mayor archaeological site](https://www.inah.gob.mx/zonas/zona-arqueologica-templo-mayor); [Palacio de Bellas Artes](https://mexicocity.cdmx.gob.mx/venues/bellas-artes/); [Museo Nacional de Antropología](https://chapultepec.cultura.gob.mx/oferta-cultural/museo-nacional-de-antropologia/)

## History and identity

Templo Mayor preserves remains of the main temple of Mexica Tenochtitlan beneath today’s historic centre. Bellas Artes and the anthropology museum show later layers of art, architecture and the country’s cultural history.

Sources: [Templo Mayor archaeological site](https://www.inah.gob.mx/zonas/zona-arqueologica-templo-mayor); [Palacio de Bellas Artes](https://mexicocity.cdmx.gob.mx/venues/bellas-artes/); [Museo Nacional de Antropología](https://chapultepec.cultura.gob.mx/oferta-cultural/museo-nacional-de-antropologia/)

## Worth seeing

### Templo Mayor

Visit the archaeological site and museum to understand the earlier city beneath the historic centre. Check the current operator ticket and access arrangements.

Sources: [Templo Mayor archaeological site](https://www.inah.gob.mx/zonas/zona-arqueologica-templo-mayor)

### Palacio de Bellas Artes

Choose between the building, its art spaces and a separately ticketed performance. An exterior look does not imply a show or museum ticket.

Sources: [Palacio de Bellas Artes](https://mexicocity.cdmx.gob.mx/venues/bellas-artes/)

### Museo Nacional de Antropología

Give this archaeology and ethnography museum a focused visit rather than treating the whole collection as a quick stop. It belongs with a Chapultepec outing.

Sources: [Museo Nacional de Antropología](https://chapultepec.cultura.gob.mx/oferta-cultural/museo-nacional-de-antropologia/)

### Bosque de Chapultepec

Use the park for outdoor time between visits. Museums and other attractions within the wider park have their own access and ticket requirements.

Sources: [Chapultepec site museum and visitor centre](https://www.mexicocity.cdmx.gob.mx/venues/chapultepec-museum/); [Museo Nacional de Antropología](https://chapultepec.cultura.gob.mx/oferta-cultural/museo-nacional-de-antropologia/)

### Zócalo and the historic-centre streets

Start with the square and a local street outing before choosing interiors. Public events or access controls can change the route on the day.

Sources: [Centro Histórico visitor guide](https://www.mexicocity.cdmx.gob.mx/locations/centro-historico/?lang=en)

## Arrival and local transport

### MEX: check your terminal

Benito Juárez International (MEX) lists Metrobús service from both terminals to the city. Choose the current onward route for your base and check luggage/payment conditions with the operator. No particular boarding door is assumed.

Sources: [AICM Metrobús](https://www.aicm.com.mx/pasajeros/servicios/prestadores-de-servicios/transportes/metrobus)

### NLU and TLC are separate arrivals

Felipe Ángeles (NLU) publishes its own city ground connections. Toluca (TLC) lists separate authorised taxi and bus services. Neither should inherit MEX directions or a generic airport-transfer allowance.

Sources: [AIFA ground connections](https://sitio.aifa.aero/conectividad/cdmx); [Toluca transportation services](https://aeropuertodetoluca.com.mx/en/transportation-services/)

### Group the centre and Chapultepec

Keep Zócalo, Templo Mayor and the Bellas Artes side of the centre on one outing; plan a separate journey for the anthropology museum and park. Check the exact entry point, not just the park name.

Sources: [Centro Histórico visitor guide](https://www.mexicocity.cdmx.gob.mx/locations/centro-historico/?lang=en); [Templo Mayor archaeological site](https://www.inah.gob.mx/zonas/zona-arqueologica-templo-mayor); [Palacio de Bellas Artes](https://mexicocity.cdmx.gob.mx/venues/bellas-artes/); [Museo Nacional de Antropología](https://chapultepec.cultura.gob.mx/oferta-cultural/museo-nacional-de-antropologia/); [Chapultepec site museum and visitor centre](https://www.mexicocity.cdmx.gob.mx/venues/chapultepec-museum/)
