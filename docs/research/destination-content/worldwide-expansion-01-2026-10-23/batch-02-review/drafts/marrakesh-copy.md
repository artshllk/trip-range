## Overview

Marrakesh rewards a plan that leaves room for the medina as well as gardens outside it. Compare an old-city base with a wider-street district before booking, and ask how you will reach the exact entrance with luggage.

Sources: [Medina in Marrakech](https://www.visitmorocco.com/en/travel/marrakech/medina); [Jardin Majorelle official site](https://www.jardinmajorelle.com/)

## History and character

A former royal capital, Marrakesh keeps much of its identity in its medina, the Koutoubia minaret and the Menara gardens. Jardin Majorelle adds a different, artist-created garden landscape beyond the old-city core.

Sources: [Medina in Marrakech](https://www.visitmorocco.com/en/travel/marrakech/medina); [Jardin Majorelle official site](https://www.jardinmajorelle.com/); [Jardin Majorelle English visitor leaflet](https://www.jardinmajorelle.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/12/Depliant-jm-en_web.pdf)

## Worth seeing

### Jemaa el-Fna

Spend time around the square and the medina streets. Performances, food and individual businesses are separate choices, not an included tour or fixed dinner price.

Sources: [Medina in Marrakech](https://www.visitmorocco.com/en/travel/marrakech/medina)

### Koutoubia

See the minaret and its setting as an exterior stop. This itinerary makes no promise of tourist access inside the mosque.

Sources: [Medina in Marrakech](https://www.visitmorocco.com/en/travel/marrakech/medina)

### Jardin Majorelle

Make a separate garden outing using the operator’s own ticket information. The site currently directs visitors to online tickets; access is not guaranteed by this guide.

Sources: [Jardin Majorelle official site](https://www.jardinmajorelle.com/)

### Menara Gardens

Visit the olive-grove and basin landscape beyond the medina. Plan the outward and return journeys instead of assuming it is beside the square.

Sources: [Medina in Marrakech](https://www.visitmorocco.com/en/travel/marrakech/medina)

## Choosing a base

### Medina

Consider a riad-style base if exploring old-city streets is the focus. Being close to the square and medina outings. Confirm the vehicle drop-off and final walking approach with the property before arrival.

Sources: [Marrakech, meet the unexpected](https://www.visitmorocco.com/en/travel/marrakesh); [Medina in Marrakech](https://www.visitmorocco.com/en/travel/marrakech/medina)

### Guéliz and Hivernage

Compare these districts outside the walls if wider streets suit your arrival plans. A different base from the medina’s lane network. Historic sights still need an onward journey; district choice does not prove a lower room price.

Sources: [Marrakech, meet the unexpected](https://www.visitmorocco.com/en/travel/marrakesh)

## Arrival and local transport

### Marrakech-Ménara (RAK)

ONDA lists airport taxis and a city-airport bus shuttle. Check the posted taxi fare or current bus route for your destination, and confirm the final approach to your accommodation. No fixed fare or timetable is quoted here.

Sources: [Marrakech-Ménara taxi and bus](https://www.onda.ma/en/Our-Airports/Marrakech-M%C3%A9nara-Airport/Services/Taxi-Bus)

### Between the medina and gardens

Keep the square and Koutoubia together. Treat Jardin Majorelle and Menara as separate outings with their own transport planning; they are not neighbouring stops on one medina walk.

Sources: [Medina in Marrakech](https://www.visitmorocco.com/en/travel/marrakech/medina); [Marrakech, meet the unexpected](https://www.visitmorocco.com/en/travel/marrakesh); [Jardin Majorelle official site](https://www.jardinmajorelle.com/)
