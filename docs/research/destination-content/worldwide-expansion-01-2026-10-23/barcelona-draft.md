## Overview

Barcelona works well when you plan by neighbourhood: Gaudí buildings in the Eixample, the lanes of the old city and art museums on separate outings. Choose the interiors that matter to you before adding up tickets.

Sources: [Eixample](https://bid.barcelonaturisme.com/wv3/en/page/19/eixample.html); [Gothic Quarter](https://bid.barcelonaturisme.com/wv3/en/page/1052/gothic-quarter.html)

## History and identity

The Gothic Quarter follows the Roman and medieval core. The later Eixample grid gave the expanding city room for the modernista buildings that still shape its streets.

Sources: [Eixample](https://bid.barcelonaturisme.com/wv3/en/page/19/eixample.html); [Gothic Quarter](https://bid.barcelonaturisme.com/wv3/en/page/1052/gothic-quarter.html)

## Worth seeing

### Sagrada Família

See Gaudí’s basilica as a planned interior visit rather than assuming you can walk straight in. Check the official visitor options for your date.

Sources: [Sagrada Família official visitor site](https://sagradafamilia.org/en/)

### Park Güell

Combine Gaudí’s designed spaces with a park outing. Check current works and ticket arrangements before deciding which parts to visit.

Sources: [Park Güell](https://parkguell.barcelona/en)

### Casa Batlló

Look for modernista detail on Passeig de Gràcia. An exterior walk and an interior visit are different choices for your budget.

Sources: [Eixample](https://bid.barcelonaturisme.com/wv3/en/page/19/eixample.html)

### Museu Picasso

Use the collection to explore Picasso’s formative years; it offers a different focus from a Gaudí architecture day.

Sources: [Picasso Collection](https://museupicassobcn.cat/en/picasso-collection)

### Museu Nacional d’Art de Catalunya

Choose this as an alternative art-museum outing. Check the current collection and entry arrangements rather than assuming another museum’s ticket includes it.

Sources: [The collection](https://www.museunacional.cat/en/collection)

## Choosing a base

### Eixample

Consider a base near a useful metro station for architecture outings and cross-city travel. May suit: Gaudí buildings and a street-grid base. Tradeoff: Check the exact block and station; Eixample is a large district.

Sources: [Eixample](https://bid.barcelonaturisme.com/wv3/en/page/19/eixample.html); [From the airport by metro](https://www.tmb.cat/en/visit-barcelona/public-transport/metro-airport)

### Gothic Quarter

Consider the old city if historic lanes are the main reason for your visit. May suit: Exploring the Roman and medieval centre on foot. Tradeoff: Check the property entrance, luggage access and noise conditions directly.

Sources: [Gothic Quarter](https://bid.barcelonaturisme.com/wv3/en/page/1052/gothic-quarter.html)

## Arrival and local transport

### Arriving at Barcelona–El Prat (BCN)

L9 Sud serves both airport terminals and connects with other metro lines. Compare the connection to your accommodation, not just the airport station. Some ordinary tickets are not valid at the airport metro gates.

Sources: [From the airport by metro](https://www.tmb.cat/en/visit-barcelona/public-transport/metro-airport)

### Getting between neighbourhoods

Group the Eixample buildings together and plan a separate old-city outing. Park Güell needs its own approach; check TMB’s route information rather than assuming every sight is an easy walk.

Sources: [Eixample](https://bid.barcelonaturisme.com/wv3/en/page/19/eixample.html); [Gothic Quarter](https://bid.barcelonaturisme.com/wv3/en/page/1052/gothic-quarter.html); [Park Güell](https://parkguell.barcelona/en); [From the airport by metro](https://www.tmb.cat/en/visit-barcelona/public-transport/metro-airport)

## Suggested short-stay outlines

These are proposed editorial sequences, not bookable tours or operator-tested schedules. Short arrival/departure days may require moving or omitting stops. No opening time or journey duration is assumed.

### 2 nights in Barcelona

- Day 1: **Casa Batlló** — Start with the exterior and the surrounding Eixample streets if arrival leaves time.
- Day 2: **Sagrada Família** — Use your confirmed entry arrangement; leave time to enjoy the interior. **Park Güell** — Make a separate connection to the park; do not assume the two Gaudí sites are adjacent.
- Day 3: **Museu Picasso** — Only with enough time before departure and confirmed access.

Sources for places and geography: [Eixample](https://bid.barcelonaturisme.com/wv3/en/page/19/eixample.html); [Sagrada Família official visitor site](https://sagradafamilia.org/en/); [Park Güell](https://parkguell.barcelona/en); [Picasso Collection](https://museupicassobcn.cat/en/picasso-collection)

### 3 nights in Barcelona

- Day 1: **Casa Batlló** — An exterior architecture walk if arrival leaves time.
- Day 2: **Sagrada Família** — Use your confirmed basilica visit. **Park Güell** — A separate park journey; leave room for a break.
- Day 3: **Museu Picasso** — Make this the main art visit.
- Day 4: **Museu Nacional d’Art de Catalunya** — An optional second museum only if departure permits; otherwise choose one museum for the stay.

Sources for places and geography: [Eixample](https://bid.barcelonaturisme.com/wv3/en/page/19/eixample.html); [Sagrada Família official visitor site](https://sagradafamilia.org/en/); [Park Güell](https://parkguell.barcelona/en); [Picasso Collection](https://museupicassobcn.cat/en/picasso-collection); [The collection](https://www.museunacional.cat/en/collection)
