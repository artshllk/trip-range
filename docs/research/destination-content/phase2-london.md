## Editorial decisions and limits

- Two nights means three calendar days; three nights means four. Arrival/departure stops are optional and must yield to actual flight times. These are editorial sequences, not operator tours or travel-time guarantees.
- Neighbourhood tradeoffs and visit pacing are planning advice, not measured hotel quality, inventory, prices, accessibility guarantees or geographic travel durations.
- No attraction ticket amounts, hotel quotes, transfer times or transfer prices are asserted. Nothing here enters deterministic trip pricing or turns an incomplete total into budget fit.
- Recheck opening days, ticket slots, room occupancy and the actual airport. Official visitor links are retained beside the relevant content and in Sources.
- Human reviewer must inspect sources, English wording, sequencing, current airport disruptions, and candidate photograph/crop before approval. The London v2 publication remains active and unchanged. Do not carry its approval into the candidate v3.
- National Gallery collection admission is described as free according to its visitor page; some exhibitions are paid. This does not remove activity uncertainty from the budget. Groups above seven have a separate official gallery booking process.
- Six operational London airports have distinct guidance. LON is never treated as Heathrow. Luton guidance uses the DART operator, not older third-party shuttle-bus copy.
