### Proposed budget-traveller sampling method

Define one adult-equivalent day before collecting prices:

| Meal slot | Fixed basket to price | Important conditions |
|---|---|---|
| Breakfast | A specified filling bakery/café item plus a standard hot drink | No assumption of free hotel breakfast; record exact portions and add-ons. |
| Lunch | A specified ready-to-eat takeaway/supermarket meal plus a stated drink | Choose meals needing no kitchen. Use single-serving prices; do not divide a whole pack's purchase price without accounting for the purchase and actual sharing. |
| Dinner | A filling casual-restaurant main, required sides and a stated drink | Include compulsory cover/service charges; keep voluntary tips identifiable. |
| Optional snack | One specified snack if included in the baseline | Declare the choice rather than hiding it inside a round-number buffer. |

Collect **three independent, comparable observations per meal slot**, spread across at least three establishments and more than one operator. Cover a practical walk/transit area relevant to each guide, rather than only a landmark café or luxury/tourist venue. National “from” prices, delivery mark-ups and loyalty-only discounts are not interchangeable with the local walk-in price.

For each observation retain the source URL, named branch/location, capture timestamp, menu effective/issue date if stated, currency, product/portion, service channel, taxes, mandatory fees and whether drinks are included. A publisher's undated menu can be recorded as accessed on a known date; do not invent its publication date. Use current branch-specific official menus/prices, or a reliable consumer study with a disclosed matching basket and date. Do not mix unmatched types as one estimate.

Collect the sample within 14 days of editorial review. Report each slot's range and median, sum comparable slot medians into a labelled **sample-based day**, and disclose any rounding and optional spending separately. Do not select only the cheapest item from each venue, promise nutritional suitability, call the result a citywide average or treat the sample range as a guaranteed price range.

The numerical result remains pending until that evidence exists. Proposed maintenance: recheck menus within 14 days of approval and review the published sample after 14 days or immediately after a known change. These intervals are editorial choices, not guarantees from the venues.
