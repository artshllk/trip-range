## Fit with the current architecture

The following are constraints found in the implementation, not proposed engineering changes.

- **Native currency and identity:** exact-currency matching only, without automatic FX. GBP/USD profiles will not change EUR-budget searches in London/New York. Use city:VIE, city:LON, city:PAR and **place:Q60**. Preserve operational airport bindings separately. See [cost identity](/Users/artshllaku/Desktop/goeksplo/goeksplo/src/domain/costs/identity.ts) and [resolution](/Users/artshllaku/Desktop/goeksplo/goeksplo/src/domain/costs/policy.ts).
- **Partial does not mean complete:** omitted components continue to use existing explicitly general/legacy planning allowances where applicable. They are not newly sourced and must not become €0 or a “fully reviewed total.” Missing flight and required-transfer protections remain unchanged.
- **Airport scope:** omit arrivalTransport in all four proposals. The model has no airport-specific dimension in a city profile; London's six airports, Paris CDG/ORY and New York EWR/JFK/LGA cannot share a checked generic transfer tariff. Existing general airport allowances are not removed or verified by this research.
- **Activities:** the only supported unit is person-night. A fixed attraction ticket is per visit and can vary with age/residency/party. Dividing one admission by two nights would misstate three-night or multi-attraction itineraries. Keep activities omitted; retain conditional prices as guide evidence pending review. No need to rewrite the model for this pilot.
- **Stay occupancy and tiers:** the current [accommodation model](/Users/artshllaku/Desktop/goeksplo/goeksplo/src/domain/accommodation.ts) uses ceil(people / 2) rooms and 70% / 100% / 150% tier scaling. These remain assumptions, not hotel-confirmed occupancy or observed prices at three accommodation standards. Tourist tax depending on adult counts cannot be exactly represented by one room-night amount; an all-in two-adult sample cannot prove arbitrary family tax totals.
- **Review and tariff dates:** profiles have one review interval and no separate travel-date tariff calendar. The [validator](/Users/artshllaku/Desktop/goeksplo/goeksplo/src/domain/costs/policy.ts) permits 1–90 days, source checks no older than 90 days, mandatory English and optional legacy Albanian. A research check does not establish that a price will remain available on a future trip.
- **Evidence types:** official tax authorities are retained in this research register. They are not mislabelled as one of the narrower runtime profile source types. No new tax component, source enum or arithmetic is needed while accommodation/arrival costs remain omitted.
- **Editorial gates:** guide changes alter pinned content digests and require the existing source review and explicit publication approval. Do not copy old Vienna/London approvals onto new cost wording. Existing photographs and approvals need no modification.
